Where It All Began

27 parks across Canada

Whistler Mountain Bike Park opened in 1999 in British Columbia — the world's first purpose-built, lift-served downhill mountain bike park at resort scale. Every park on this guide is downstream of that experiment. Gravity Logic, founded by Whistler's original trail crew in 2007, has since designed six of the 27 parks listed here — and consulted on dozens more worldwide.

Provinces with no lift-served parks: Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland & Labrador, Northwest Territories, and Nunavut have no confirmed lift-served mountain bike parks. The concentration of Canadian lift infrastructure falls primarily in mountainous BC and the Laurentian ski corridor of Québec.
